INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Cyberspace Security at the University of Science and Technology of China. I work on efficient and secure networked systems, with an emphasis on practical mechanisms that balance performance, scalability, and security.

My recent work spans workload-adaptive cache eviction, programmable data-center transport, and security mechanisms for information-centric networking.

Research Interests

Network Transmission

High-performance data-center networking, RDMA transport, and programmable in-network mechanisms.

Network & Content Security

Information-centric networking security, content poisoning defense, and interest-flooding mitigation.

Selected Research Projects

RSM · Workload-Preference-Aware Cache Eviction

Designed a middle layer that represents workload-level eviction preferences and decouples model optimization from latency-critical victim selection.

Cache Systems

NetRT · Retransmission Offloading for RDMA

Explored programmable-switch-assisted selective retransmission between top-of-rack switches for resilient data-center transport.

Data Center Networks

NDN Security · SLP, HPR-DS, and RPBV

Developed probing-based content-poisoning defense, hybrid mitigation of interest flooding, and reputation-based probabilistic batch verification.

Network Security

MCM · Multi-Faceted and Continuous Mitigation of Cache Pollution

Ongoing research on cache-pollution defense using anomaly detection, group similarity recognition, and proactive cache admission.
